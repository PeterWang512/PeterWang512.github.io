I am a final year PhD at CMU, advised by Jun-Yan Zhu. These days, I am also working closely with Richard Zhang, Alexei (Alyosha) Efros, Aaron Hertzmann, and David Bau. I am generally interested in image synthesis and understanding deep neural networks. During undergrad, I worked at BAIR with Oliver Wang, Richard Zhang, Andrew Owens and Alexei (Alyosha) Efros. In the past, I have worked on image forensics, and condensed matter physics. My research is supported by Google PhD Fellowship, Snap Research Fellowship, and Uber Presidential Fellowship.
